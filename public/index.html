How the numbers work

Cost is the raw price plus grading fee plus shipping and insurance.

Expected profit weighs every outcome: chance of a 10 × the 10 price, chance of the near grade × its price, and everything else at the Grade 8 price, all after selling fees, minus cost. For PSA the near grade is 9; for BGS and CGC it's 9.5.

10 chance comes from the PSA pop report when a card is matched (tagged pop), otherwise your default (default). A dashed tag means the match to PSA's list was uncertain, so it isn't used. Type in the box to set your own (yours). Pop data only covers PSA; BGS and CGC use your defaults.

Break-even is how often the card must come back a 10 for you not to lose money. Most room above break-even ranks by the gap between the card's 10 chance and that break-even point: the safest bets, not the biggest.

Sales/yr is PriceCharting's yearly sales volume. For cards from PokemonPriceTracker it's the number of graded eBay sales they recorded. A great return on a card that sells twice a year is hard to cash out.
